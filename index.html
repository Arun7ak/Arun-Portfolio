About Me

ArunKumar K is a Data Scientist with 1+ year of professional experience at Prowesstics, specializing in machine learning systems, automated ML pipelines, and AI-driven analytics solutions on large-scale datasets.

He built an end-to-end ML pipeline for churn prediction and segmentation on 30L+ records, achieving 89.3% accuracy with optimized XGBoost. He also developed an LLM-based recommendation system and RAG-powered AI chatbot using LLaMA 4 Maverick and Azure OpenAI, automated ML model selection with Apache Airflow (reducing manual effort by ~60%), and a YOLOv8-based retail footfall counting system.

Before joining Prowesstics, he independently built 10 personal projects spanning recommendation systems, predictive analytics, computer vision, and NLP. His technical interests span machine learning systems, generative AI, MLOps pipelines, and data-driven business decision frameworks. He holds a B.E. in Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering from Velammal Engineering College, Chennai, with an A+ grade.
